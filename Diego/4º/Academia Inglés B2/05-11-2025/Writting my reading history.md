I have spent much time in my life reading many things like social media, novel games and, obviously, books. Reading has been one of my favorite hobbies along my life, but, when did I start reading?

As far as I know, my parents told me I learned to read very fast, and perhaps that was the reason I started reading books when I was 4 years old. I can remember when I read in class to all my classmates because of my reading ability when I was 4 or 5 years old. At home, I remember reading many books too such as "El Diario de Greg", "Gormiti" and some academic books. Even my parents told me they saw me once reading a relativity theory book by myself.

When I started secondary school, I continued reading, but gradually I was reading rather less than when I was a child. I don't know what the reasons were, but I lost the illusion of reading a book. It doesn't mean I stopped reading as I also enjoyed many books like "Mentira" and "Verdad", which had to be readen in school for an exam.

Nowadays, I haven't read any book for years and I don't find any topic that could interest me at the moment. However, I'm now playing a videogame saga whose name is Phoenix Wright, which is a novel based game about being an attorney defending his culprit which is accused of assasination, and the only interaction you can make is reading the story the game tells you, so I think it can be also considered kinda a book.
