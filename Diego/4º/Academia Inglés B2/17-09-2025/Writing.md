Some people think success is everything. For others, happiness is all they need. In your opinion, what’s more important, success or happiness?


During all history, many people state that you can live with success and not looking on happiness, and there are also people that states the opposite. In my opinion, people whose mind is obsessed with only one of these perspectives have a wrong point of view about success and happiness. 

On one hand, success without happiness would mean living a life without making any effort. For example, rich people always have success in life because of their echonomical situation but, because of that, when they have to solve any problem, they arent able to make any effort in order to reach their goals, and that's sad.

On the other hand, I think that a happy life without "real" success can't be possible because that would mean to be a life without any strong goal to reach.  This lifestyle can feel so painful for someone who don't have any motivation in life

In summary, happiness and success can't live without each other, and the only way to have success in life is to keep making consistent effort day a day and reaching slowly your objectives in order to be happy.
