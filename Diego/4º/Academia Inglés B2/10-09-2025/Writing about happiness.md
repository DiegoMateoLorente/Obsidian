I’ve been thinking for the last days about what I should write here to define happiness. I was thinking about the discussion Ana and I had last week about what we think about happiness and, although we had good and similar ideas, I think there’s still something left to cover the real meaning of it. On one hand, Ana said that happiness can be defined as having a healthy and controlled routine, and spending time with your family. On the other hand, I said that I could relate happiness with spending time with the people you love, always having your own ideas clear and, overall, being at peace with oneself. After that, Aristotle’s version made me think about what Ana, Aristotle and I have in common, and I was reflecting on another definition of happiness. Finally, I concluded that, for me, happiness means acting in consequence of your own reflection. For example, when you reach any goal in your life acting in that way, you feel very proud of yourself and then you reach the maximum level of happiness.
